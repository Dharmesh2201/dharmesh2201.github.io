Sheet M-01 · Pi Robots Technology, Mumbai

Pipe-inspection quadcopter

A drone that flies inside confined pipelines and films the walls.

As Lead Design Engineer I owned the mechanical side of the drone: body, frame and accessories, from first sketch to released drawings. Inside a pipe there's no GPS and the airframe will hit the wall, so protection drove most decisions. The ducts keep all four props inside the disc, so contact with the wall doesn't foul a blade.

  • Designed a carbon-fiber protective chassis with ducted rotors and a honeycomb-lattice guard.
  • Packaged brushless motors, a dual visible/infrared camera and lighting into a compact payload bay with defined interfaces.
  • Released 2D drawings for manufacturing and kept the BOM at 100% component traceability.
  • Ran hands-on build, assembly and test cycles, then worked root cause with manufacturing to cut rework.

Sheet M-02 · Thermal research, published in ISES SWC 2023 Proceedings

Waste-to-Best solar water heater

A 100 L thermosiphon heater built from PVC pipe and upcycled PET bottles, tested against a commercial collector.

The goal was a heater cheap enough to build from waste material that still performs like a commercial evacuated tube collector (ETC). I designed and built the 2 × 1 m collector on a mild steel frame, with reflective foil and a removable winter glazing layer for colder months.

Three absorber geometries went head to head against an ETC. Less water held in the collector (dead volume) means faster thermosiphon flow and a shorter heat-up. Model 2, a 32 mm pipe inside a 40 mm pipe, won.

  • Drawings, BOM and documentation in SolidWorks and CATIA.
  • Test data analyzed in MATLAB.

Sheet M-03 · Research project, La Fondation Dassault Systèmes

Solar dryer and disinfection dome

A hemispherical collector that dries and disinfects food, and keeps going after sunset.

Open-air drying leaves grain, spices and fruit exposed to dust, insects, birds and UV, and never gets hot enough to disinfect. This design scaled from a 1 kg countertop unit to a 4 m dome holding 1,000 kg. I produced the fully dimensioned CATIA models, construction drawings and GD&T for the collector and tray-support structure.

  • Designed a heat exchanger subsystem that heats the chamber at night, so the dryer runs 24-hour cycles.
  • Ran a 32-channel data logger campaign at 8 heights under the dome to map forced and natural convection.
  • Helped assemble and field-test it on grapes, onions and neem leaves against open-air and shed drying.

Cast aluminum throttle body

Designing a part around how the metal actually solidifies.

With a team of four I redesigned a sand-cast aluminum throttle body over three CAD iterations, removing thick sections that solidify slowly and cause defects.

  • Laid out the gating system and part orientation to push solidification toward the risers.
  • Simulated mold filling and solidification, then moved cold shuts out of the main body.

Mobility tricycle

A hand-powered tricycle for riders with physical disabilities.

I redesigned the drive system components using kinematic analysis and force calculations to get more of the rider's effort into the wheels. Each version was built and tested by hand before the next change.

The same design language carried into a second product, a wheelchair that converts to a stretcher with a reclining, dual-position frame. Both received granted design patents in India.
